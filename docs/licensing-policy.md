# Tetrion licensing policy for mathema

Effective 2026-09-07. This policy accompanies the Business Source
License 1.1 that mathema is distributed under (`LICENSE.md` in the
repository); the license, including its Additional Use Grant, governs,
and this policy states how Tetrion Ltd applies it.

## Free-use thresholds (the Additional Use Grant's floors)

Production use is free of charge for any Organisation meeting **any**
of the grant's conditions:

1. total Organisation revenue below **USD 10,000,000** in the most
   recently completed fiscal year;
2. use in connection with **at most three code repositories** across
   the Organisation;
3. research, teaching, personal, or other **non-commercial** use;
4. **evaluation**, for up to **90 days** from the Organisation's
   first use.

"Organisation" is defined in the grant: the legal entity together
with every entity that controls it, is controlled by it, or is under
common control with it. The grant never permits providing third
parties a product or service having substantially the same function
as mathema or as a Tetrion product built on it.

## Cure period

An Organisation that ceases to qualify (a revenue floor crossed at a
fiscal year end, a fourth repository, an evaluation running past 90
days) has **60 days** from the date it ceased to qualify to either
obtain a commercial license from Tetrion or end production use.
Good-faith production use during that window is treated as licensed.
Rights under the license terminate only when the window closes with
neither having happened.

## Conversion schedule

Each released version's Change Date is **four years from that
version's release date**, computed at release time and written into
that release's LICENSE.md. On its Change Date a version converts to
the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later** and every
restriction above stops applying to that version. (AGPL-3.0-or-later
satisfies the license's Covenants of Licensor: it is compatible with
GPL Version 3.0, a later version of GPL Version 2.0.)
